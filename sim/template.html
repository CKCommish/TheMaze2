Simulation settings

Restarting resets the story. In the real product the writer is Claude and each shot is a 15-second H3 Max clip with sound; here a template writer and storyboard cards keep it free.

How it works. One beat is 45 seconds: three 15-second shots (setup, action, turn). The vote opens with 30 seconds left and closes with 25 left, so the winning option’s first clip has 25 seconds to render before the next beat starts. If a clip is late, an establishing shot plays and the real clip cuts in. If nobody votes, option A wins. The winning option is the one with the highest total gift value.
