# Story bible (working draft)

Everything here is a starting point. The code reads the bible from `src/core/bible.ts`; `story/bible.json` overrides it (that is where `npm run cast` writes the locked reference photos).

## The show

**THE MAZE.** Port Marrow, a rain-soaked coastal city run by shipping money and old debts. Rae Solano, a courier who just stole the wrong bag, has one night to turn it into a way out. The audience decides how.

Why this frame: everyone understands "one bad night in a crime city"; it produces natural three-way choices; it is a sandbox (the city is the maze, every beat is a fork); and it plays to what the video model renders best (rain, neon, harbors, freeways, vast empty skylines).

## The look (injected into every prompt)

> Cinematic photoreal live-action. Shot on a large-format digital cinema camera with anamorphic lenses, shallow depth of field, natural motion blur, 24 fps. Overcast coastal light, wet asphalt, sodium-vapor orange and cold cyan neon, drifting sea mist, vast desolate skylines and container yards in the distance. Muted teal-and-amber grade, deep blacks, restrained contrast, subtle film grain. Grounded, weighty physics; no cartoon exaggeration, no text overlays, no logos, no watermarks.

The Death Stranding note: wide, lonely landscapes with a small figure; weather as a character; long quiet shots between bursts of action.

## Cast (locked looks)

- **Rae Solano** (protagonist). Late twenties, wiry, 5'8", light-brown skin, sharp jaw, cropped black hair buzzed short on the left, a thin pale scar through her left eyebrow, dark watchful eyes, a silver ring on her right thumb. Always: faded grey-green bomber with a worn orange lining, black tee, black jeans, scuffed white sneakers, black canvas sling bag.
- **Teo Marsh** (ally). Around forty, broad, pale weathered skin, greying reddish beard, receding hairline, tired grey eyes, faded anchor tattoo on the right forearm. Navy fisherman's sweater with a hole at the collar, oil-stained work trousers, steel-toe boots. Runs a boat shed on the inlet.
- **Detective Ana Vasquez** (rival). Mid-forties, tall, olive skin, dark hair in a tight low bun, strong brows, a mole under the right eye, an unhurried stare. Charcoal trench over a white shirt, badge on a chain, black leather gloves.
- **Milo Kessler** (rival). Fifties, lean and immaculate, silver hair swept back, rimless glasses, gold signet ring. Slate-blue suit, open collar, slim black umbrella. The fixer who wants the bag.

Each gets an identity pack (front, three-quarter, profile, full body) made once and never regenerated. The wardrobe is unmistakable on purpose: it survives even when the face drifts.

## The world state the writer tracks

Location · time of day · weather · **heat** (0–5, police attention) · cash · allies · inventory · open threads (max 6) · the last 8 beat synopses. Heat rises with violence and chaos and falls with stealth or deals; cash changes with jobs, bribes and losses.

## Locations to start with

Pier 9 container yard · Skyline Freeway (elevated over the harbor) · Teo's boat shed on Marrow inlet · Lantern Row night market · The Meridian casino on the hill · Delgado's chop shop under the freeway · the Marrow Grand Hotel rooftop at dawn.

## Choice design

Every vote offers three options: **one careful, one bold, one wild**. Labels 2–5 words ("Answer the phone", "Ram the gate", "Pull the fire alarm"). Each must be doable inside 45 seconds and change the state. The cliffhanger is a frozen decision moment (a ringing phone, a fork in the road, an open elevator) so any option can follow. Nobody votes → the careful option (A).

## Content rules

- Standard: no gore, blood, torture, sexual content, drug use, real people, real brands, logos or on-screen text. Weapons may be shown without graphic injury.
- **TikTok LIVE mode (default):** additionally no firearms, explosives or knives on screen, no physical fights even non-graphic. Threats verbal; violence off screen or implied by sound and aftermath. Chases, crashes, break-ins, heists, deals, betrayals and escapes are fine.
- Every generated script goes through the writer's rules; before launch add a text classifier on every shot (the three AI streams that got banned were banned for generated dialogue).

## Season structure (suggested)

Nights are episodes. A night runs ~3 hours (240 beats). Each night ends on a hard cliffhanger; the next starts by resolving it. Every 4–6 beats something pays off; the bag's contents are revealed in pieces over weeks. Recurring gifts from viewers could later unlock named "cameo" extras, a cheaper monetization than selling votes.
